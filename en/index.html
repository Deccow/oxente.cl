About us

A little piece of Brazil on Los Leones

Oxente was born to share Brazilian cooking in the heart of Providencia: generous, home-style dishes, coffee to start your day, and that warm Brazilian hospitality that keeps you coming back.

Drop by for breakfast, take your time over lunch, or grab something delicious to go.
